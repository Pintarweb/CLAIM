# Phase 1 Implementation Plan: The Pilot Acquisition (Zero-to-Low Budget)

**Objective:** Acquire 20 high-quality travel agency pilot partners in Malaysia.
**Budget Constraints:** Bootstrapped (Under $50 total spend).
**Core Philosophy:** Sweat equity over ad spend. We will use highly targeted, organic, and direct outreach methods leveraging free tiers of powerful software.

---

## 🛠️ Required Stack (The "Bootstrapper's Toolbox")
All of these have robust free tiers perfect for this phase:
1. **Lead Generation:** Apollo.io (Free tier gives you email credits) / MATTA Public Directory / LinkedIn.
2. **Email Automation:** Brevo (Already set up - free tier allows 300 emails/day).
3. **Meeting Scheduler:** Brevo Meetings (Creates a seamless booking page that syncs automatically with your Brevo CRM).
4. **Design:** Canva (Free tier) or your existing image generation setup for social graphics.
5. **CRM/Tracking:** Brevo Sales CRM (Free built-in Kanban board to track leads from uncontacted to Pilot signed).

## 🧠 Required Skills (Your Roles)
*   **The Researcher (Data Mining):** Scraping directories and LinkedIn for the right contacts.
*   **The Copywriter:** Crafting cold emails and LinkedIn posts that don't sound like generic corporate spam.
*   **The Consultant (Sales):** Getting on 15-minute calls not to "sell software," but to consult on their LHDN compliance workflow.

---

## 📅 The 4-Week Execution Plan

### WEEK 1: The "Hit List" & Setup 
*Goal: System foundation and a list of 200 highly qualified targets.*

*   **Day 1-2: Tool Setup**
    *   Set up a **Brevo Meetings** link: `15-Min: Mileage Compliance Workflow Review`.
    *   Configure your **Brevo Sales CRM**: Create columns (stages) for *Lead, Contacted, Kit Downloaded, Call Booked, Pilot Signed*.
*   **Day 3-5: Data Mining (The Grunt Work)**
    *   Search the MATTA website for registered agencies. 
    *   Use LinkedIn to search for "Owner", "Managing Director", or "Finance Manager" at those specific companies.
    *   Use Apollo.io (free extension) to find their direct email addresses.
    *   *Target:* Populate your Brevo CRM with 200 qualified leads as "Lead" status.

### WEEK 2: The "Trojan Horse" Organic Launch
*Goal: Create inbound interest using your pre-built '2026 Audit-Ready Kit'.*

*   **Day 6: LinkedIn Authority Posting**
    *   Publish a post on your personal LinkedIn: *"LHDN audits are shifting focus to travel & mileage in 2026. I've compiled a free Audit-Ready SOP Kit specifically for Malaysian Travel Agencies. Comment 'KIT' and I'll DM it to you."*
    *   This algorithmic hack (asking for comments) boosts organic reach.
*   **Day 7-10: Guerrilla Distribution**
    *   Join Malaysian SME, HR, and Travel/Tourism groups on Facebook and LinkedIn.
    *   Do not spam your link. Leave a helpful post summarizing *one* thing from the kit, and say: *"I have a full folder of these SOP templates if anyone wants them, just let me know."*
    *   When they ask, send them the link to your SuccessState page where they have to trade their email for the download—boom, inbound lead automatically added to your Brevo.

### WEEK 3: Direct Cold Outreach (The Engine)
*Goal: Activate Brevo to reach out to your 200 scraped leads.*

*   **Day 11-12: Brevo Campaign Setup**
    *   Set up a simple 2-step automated sequence in Brevo for all contacts currently in your CRM "Lead" column (Text only, no images, so it lands in the Primary inbox):
*   **Day 13: Send Email 1 (The Value Add)**
    *   *Subject:* `Quick question re: LHDN mileage compliance`
    *   *Body:* "Hi [Name], I'm Yusmarin. We are building a tool to automate the 'Three-Way Match' for travel agency mileage claims to ensure 100% LHDN compliance. I recently put together a free 2026 Audit-Ready Kit with SOPs and templates. Would you find it useful if I sent it over?"
*   **Day 14-15: Send Email 2 (The Pilot Pitch - For those who engaged/replied)**
    *   *Body:* "Glad the kit was helpful. I'm currently onboarding 10 local agencies for our closed Pilot Program for ClaimFlow. It completely automates the manual work outlined in that kit. Open to a 10 min call to see if it fits [Agency Name]'s workflow? **https://meet.brevo.com/yusmarin-samsudin/intro**"

### WEEK 4: Closing & Onboarding
*Goal: Convert calls into active pilot users.*

*   **Day 16-20: The Discovery Calls**
    *   Your goal on these calls is 80% listening, 20% pitching. Ask them how they do it *today*, how much time it takes, and what hurts the most.
    *   If there's a fit, say: *"Let me set up an account for you right now. It's completely free during the pilot, I just want your harsh feedback."*
*   **Day 21+: Concierge Onboarding**
    *   Do not just send them a login link. Get on a Zoom call and literally set up their first driver/route in the system with them. 
    *   White-glove service is how you win evangelists in the early days.

## 💡 Pro-Tips for the Lean Startup Marketer
1. **Never sound like a marketing department.** Your emails should sound like one founder talking to another founder. Plain text, casual tone, straight to the point.
2. **Handle Rejections as Data.** If they say "no," ask "why?" Is it pricing? Lack of integration? Don't see the value? That feedback is gold for adjusting your product roadmap.
3. **Leverage the 'Exclusive' Angle.** Don't frame it as "We are a beta software, please test us." Frame it as "We are taking a limited number of founding partners who will get to shape the tool and lock in lifetime discounts." It shifts the power dynamic.
